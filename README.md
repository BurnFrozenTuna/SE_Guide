# SE_Guide — Fish_In_the_Canvas

**An accurate, continuously-corrected, community-maintained guide to the StoryEngine Canvas — for creators who are new to it.**
A node-by-node walkthrough of what each node is, what you actually write inside it, and how it behaves under the hood, with screenshots throughout. Available in **English and Japanese**, and kept in step with the platform as it changes.

**📖 Read it here:** https://burnfrozentuna.github.io/SE_Guide/ — 日本語版: https://burnfrozentuna.github.io/SE_Guide/ja/

---

## What this is

A hands-on starter guide to the StoryEngine Canvas, written by **Frozen_Tuna** while actually building on it. It covers the story, character, macro-prompt (lorebook), image, variable, trigger and achievement nodes, plus image-tag categorizing, preset notes, Advanced Settings and the publish page.

- **Two editions**, switchable from the sidebar tabs: **English** and **日本語**.
- Much of what's in here was reported, tested, or translated by other creators — they're credited in the **Contributors** section at the bottom of the guide.
- A companion page, **[Fish_In_the_Toolbox](https://burnfrozentuna.github.io/Fish_In_the_Toolbox/)**, collects platform-agnostic techniques (GPT typography, external image hosting, in-browser tools) that don't belong in a StoryEngine-specific guide.

## This is **not** official — read this part too

It is **not written, reviewed, or endorsed by StoryEngine**, and it is not affiliated with the platform in any way. Parts of the platform's internals are deliberately undisclosed, so some of this is worked out by hand, some is guesswork, and **some of it is simply wrong**. Numbers — file-size caps especially — get changed by the platform without notice.

**Do not take this as a source of truth.** Keep the Canvas open, check what it tells you, and **believe the Canvas over this document every single time.** Treat every number here as "last confirmed," not as fact.

## Found a mistake?

Please tell me. Open an [issue](../../issues) with what's wrong and, if you have one, a screenshot. Corrections are genuinely welcome — that's how most of what's in here got worked out in the first place.

**Not comfortable in English?** Open the issue in your own language and say which one it is. Korean, English, Japanese, and Spanish are all fine, and I'll do my best to reply in the language you wrote in — a rough machine translation is welcome too. I'd rather have the report than have you skip it over the language.
